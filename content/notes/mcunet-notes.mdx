date: "2025-03-05"

---

MCUNet jointly optimizes the NN architecture (NAS) and the inference engine (TinyEngine). The key metric: peak memory usage, not just model size. Flash stores weights; SRAM is the bottleneck. INT8 quantization is essentially mandatory.
