## The challenge

Running neural networks on resource-constrained hardware is genuinely hard. When that hardware is also flying at 30km/h, the constraints multiply.

## Why UAVs?

UAVs (drones) need real-time inference for tasks like:
- Obstacle detection
- Object tracking
- Anomaly detection in inspection tasks

## The hardware constraints

A typical microcontroller used in UAVs:
- ~256KB RAM
- ~1MB Flash
- No GPU, no FPU sometimes

This means standard PyTorch models are completely out of the question.

## What works

- **Quantization**: INT8 instead of FP32 — 4x smaller, minimal accuracy loss
- **Pruning**: remove redundant weights
- **Knowledge distillation**: train a tiny student model from a large teacher
- **Architecture search**: MobileNet, MCUNet, EfficientNet-Lite

## MCUNet

MCUNet is a framework that co-designs the neural architecture and inference engine for microcontrollers. It's been a big part of my research focus.

The key insight: optimize the *memory footprint* of the entire inference graph, not just the model size.
